# 30-second demo video script

Target: **≤ 30 s** (verify the current limit in the Space Apps rules). Screen recording of the
running app + voice-over. ~70 words of narration fits 30 s. **English subtitles are required** by the Space Apps submission guide — burn them into the video (not only an .srt).

| Time | Screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | Title card: **IGNIS** — Intelligent Guidance for Navigating Ignition in Space | "Fire in a spacecraft is still one of the fastest ways to lose a crew." |
| 4–10 s | Overview page: 24 investigations, filters | "NASA has run decades of fire experiments in microgravity — twenty-four investigations, thousands of files." |
| 10–17 s | Scenario ranker → choose **Lunar base** → ranked list with "why" | "Pick a habitat, like a Moon base, and we rank the experiments that matter — and explain why." |
| 17–23 s | Chat: ask a question → cited passages [1][2] → click a citation | "Ask anything; AI search finds the answer in NASA's own documents — down to the page." |
| 23–30 s | Condition map, Moon-base marker, empty region for solids | "And the gap: no real fabric or plastic sample was ever tested at Moon-base oxygen levels — only a burner emulating plastic, and it kept burning." |

Recording tips: 1920×1080, browser zoom 110 %, hide bookmarks bar, pre-load the pages (first
search takes ~7 s while the embedding model loads), use cached questions from `ai/examples.md`.
