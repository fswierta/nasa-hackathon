## Documents cited in the app and the submission

- NASA, *A Researcher's Guide to: International Space Station Combustion Science* (2015), NP-2015-10-034-JSC —
  misconceptions about spacecraft fires (p. 35). https://www.nasa.gov/missions/station/a-researchers-guide-to-combustion-science/
- ACME/BRE Final Report (1 Feb. 2022), PSI-20 document `PSI-20_Reports_Final_20220201.pdf` — burner tests at
  34 % O2 / 8.2 psia and 26.5 % O2 / 10.2 psia (pp. 24–26, Table 3 p. 122).
- P. Ferkul, K. Kralik, Y.-T. Liao, M. Johnston, "Material Flammability at Lunar and Martian Gravity",
  57th Lunar and Planetary Science Conference (2026), NASA NTRS 20260001966 (slide 17).
- Olson & Ferkul, "Goldilocks" flammability zone — as described in the PSI-62 metadata (DOI 10.60555/e9xp-ex83).
- NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, "Flame in Freefall" challenge page:
  https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/flame-in-freefall/
- NASA Spinoff: Fire Detector (https://spinoff.nasa.gov/node/9407), Fireman's Air Tanks
  (https://spinoff.nasa.gov/node/9582), New Technology for the Fire Attire (https://spinoff.nasa.gov/node/9918).
- NIOSH, Carbon dioxide — Immediately Dangerous to Life or Health (IDLH) value: https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/idlh/124389.html
- Investigator documents in each PSI investigation's "Science Documents" and "Reports" (93 PDF/Word files),
  cited in the app by file and page.
